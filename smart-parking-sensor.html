HOW IT CONNECTS

From Parking Space to Monitoring Platform

The sensor detects the occupancy status of an individual parking space and transmits the status through wireless IoT connectivity to a gateway, cloud platform or parking management system.

THE PROBLEM

Know Which Parking Spaces Are Available

Traditional parking management provides limited visibility into individual parking spaces. A smart parking sensor enables real-time occupancy monitoring, helping operators understand parking availability and manage spaces more efficiently.
