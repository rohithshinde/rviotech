HOW IT CONNECTS

From Door Status to Remote Monitoring

The sensor detects whether a door is open or closed and transmits the status through wireless connectivity to a gateway, application or monitoring platform.

THE PROBLEM

Know When Doors Are Open or Closed

Manual checking of doors across facilities, equipment rooms and restricted areas can be time-consuming. A connected door sensor provides remote visibility of door status and event activity.
